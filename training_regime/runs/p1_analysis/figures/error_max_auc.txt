Area under the curve -- error_max_pushforward_vs_fbptt.png
======================================================================

Curves: err_max (max absolute error along the beam) vs time, taken from
the 'curves' block of runs/p1_pushforward/metrics.json (Pushforward) and
runs/p1_bptt/metrics.json (TBPTT) -- the exact arrays the figure plots.
Composite trapezoidal rule on the shared uniform grid (dt = 0.03 s), with
the last 2 samples dropped exactly as the figure drops them.
Integration window: t = 0.06 .. 4.86 s  (161 samples, span 4.80 s)

----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. LINEAR AREA   int e(t) dt        <- the integral in the usual sense
----------------------------------------------------------------------
   units: (error units) x s

   Pushforward  5.726291e-04     time-average e = 1.192977e-04
   TBPTT        4.731336e-03     time-average e = 9.856951e-04

   Ratio    TBPTT / Pushforward = 8.262 x
   Absolute difference          = 4.158707e-03

   On a linear scale the integral is dominated by the late-time plateau
   (t > 1 s), where both errors are largest. The early ramp (1e-8..1e-5)
   contributes essentially nothing to it.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
2. LOG AREA   int log10(e(t)) dt    <- the area as it LOOKS on this figure
----------------------------------------------------------------------
   The y-axis is logarithmic, so the visible area between a curve and the
   bottom of the plot is not metric 1. This is that area. Values are
   negative because e < 1 over the whole rollout.
   Window t = 0.09 .. 4.86 s (span 4.77 s): the t = 0.06 s sample is excluded
   because err_max is exactly 0 there and log10(0) is undefined.

   Pushforward   -19.7132     mean log10 e = -4.1327  ->  geometric mean e = 7.3665e-05
   TBPTT         -15.6546     mean log10 e = -3.2819  ->  geometric mean e = 5.2254e-04

   Gap in log area (TBPTT - Pushforward) = +4.0586
   Mean vertical gap on the figure       = 0.8509 decade
   -> TBPTT's error is on average 7.09 x higher than Pushforward's.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
3. CROSS-CHECK against the pointwise ratio
----------------------------------------------------------------------
   Same pair, TBPTT/Pushforward computed timestep by timestep (this is
   what error_max_ratio_fbptt_over_pushforward.png plots), over the same
   160 samples:

     arithmetic mean 8.387 x   geometric mean 7.062 x   median 8.034 x
     range 1.029 x .. 22.093 x   (Pushforward is ahead at every timestep)

   The geometric mean (7.06 x) matches the log-area result (7.09 x) to within
   rounding -- they are the same quantity, the log area just weights the
   endpoints by half (trapezoid rule). That agreement is the sanity check
   that the areas above are right.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
WHICH NUMBER TO QUOTE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
   Linear area (metric 1): 'total error accumulated over the rollout' ->
   TBPTT is 8.3 x worse. Driven by the plateau, so it is really a
   statement about late-time behaviour.

   Log area (metric 2): 'typical multiplicative gap, every decade counted
   equally' -> TBPTT is 7.1 x worse. More robust, less sensitive to the
   few worst timesteps.

   Both agree in direction and are close in magnitude (8.3 x vs 7.1 x).

   CAUTION -- do not mix these with summary.txt's numbers:

   (a) runs/p1_analysis/summary.txt quotes '4.09x mean / 3.25x geometric
       mean' for its HEADLINE PAIR, which is a different pair of runs:
       the archived 'rollout val' ones (Archives_runs/p1_pushforward_old
       vs Archives_runs/p1_bptt_old). This figure plots the 'loss val'
       pair -- p1_pushforward (which summary.txt lists as 'pushforward,
       no PINN (loss val)') and p1_bptt -- for which the gap is larger.

       gives final = 1.3847e-03 / peak = 2.5221e-03. The p1_pushforward
